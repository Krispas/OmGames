250hp. A large flying automaton. It hovers 1 block above ground, it has squished reinforced deepslate for bottom, then block of polished basalt as a column then another squished reinforced deepslate. There are 2 redstone blocks acting as eyes on the basalt. The basalt itself is 2x2x2 blocks large, scale everything according to that, the deepslates are a little bit wider, acting as lids. Above the upport deepslate is a stone_brick rod. (squished stone bricks, but still 1 block high), this connects to a propeller made out of spruce_planks, the propeler rotates quickly.

It has multiple attacks and two phases, the phase 2 activates when reaching 33% of health. The second phase alters some of its attacks. When second phase is reached, anything it currently is in middle of gets interrupted, a deep growl and particles around it are generated, then returns back to its idle state.

1. Missile - The guard starts prepping a misile in front of its face, aiming for the nearest player, after 3 seconds, the spot is locked, particles on the, place, after 1 more seconds, the missile is launched, exploding upon impact. 2 second cooldown
2. Shockwave - The guard starts firing a laster towards the ground, after 3 seconds, 1-3 shockwaves are released, each second appart, these are exactly the same shockwaves as overdrive_spawner has
3. Walls - Simillar to shockwave attack, however it is telegraphed by explosion particles around the guard. There are again 1-3 shockwaves, however they are walls which cannot be jumped over, instead there is a 30 degree hole each 60 degrees, the rotation for each shockwave is random and there are 3 seconds between the shockwaves. There is 5 second cooldown after this.
4. Spawn - The guard flies block up, then crashes itself into ground, this should take 2 seconds. This will spawn 1-2 monsters, falling from the ceiling (explosion particles mask their spawn in). Cannot execute this attack for another 30 seconds. It has 3 second cooldown. It spawns Bedrock Walkers. The walkers however have only half their normal health.
5. Reposition - The guard flies to a random position in the room, leaving small poison clouds behind it, which vanish in 5 seconds. Has 3 second cooldown.

Second phase changes:
1. Missile - Right after shooting the first missile, the second one starts getting armed.
2. Shockwave - there will be 3-5 shockwaves instead of 1-3
3. Walls - The wall safe spots will be about 20% smaller
4. Spawn - Instead of Bedrock Walkers, spawn Rotting Soldiers
5. Reposition - Instead of poison clouds, create wither clouds.
