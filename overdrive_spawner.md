A giant immobile spawner (5x5x5 blocks). 150hp

It has 3 attacks.

1. Spawn - The spawner takes 3 seconds to charge up (particles), then it spawns mobs (rotate it 360 degrees with a small 0.1 block jump as an animation, add rotations to other axis to make it more lively.) Spawn mobs based on a pool, which is part of the .yml file together with other values. It can spawn splinters, hoglins, zombies, vindicators, husks, slimes. There is 7 second cooldown after this attack.
2. Jump - The spawner has half a second to ready for a jump, then jumps 3 blocks into the air and then crashes down, creating a circular particle showckwave which expands throughout the room. The shockwave deals 6 damage and can be jumped over. The attack has 1 second cooldown. If boss's health is above 50%, any other attack can be executed after it, if it is under 50%, then there is 70% that the next attack will be another jump.
3. X blast - spawner will randomly rotate on y axis with a small jump to help it move beforehand (1 second), then it charges for 2 seconds, showing warning particles in the blast zone.  It will then shoot 4 beams from each 4 of its sides. 2 second cooldown after this attack.
