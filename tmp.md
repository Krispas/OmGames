# Halls Modifier Ideas

1. **Glass Bones** - Players deal more melee damage, but all incoming trap damage is increased.
2. **Spoiled Air** - Healing from food is reduced, but coin drops are slightly more common.
5. **Scrap Bloom** - Scrap drops are doubled, but the coin quota is increased for the floor.
6. **Thin Floor** - Hole traps are more common, but any bridged hole generates wider bridges.
8. **Rust Rot** - Weapons lose temporary durability faster, but armor receives a small resistance bonus.
10. **Mercy Cache** - The first ghost created on the floor drops a small coin cache
